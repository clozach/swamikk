# Private Sessions and independent public resources

This prepared content import serves the 7 September 2026 request to keep the membership site independent of Anahata except for deliberate outbound links. Importing the helper does not fetch, write, publish, email, or book anything.

The content record preserves the published Private Sessions copy and contact. Its two image cards were visually checked: a single 60-minute session is NZD120 and five are NZD575; a single 90-minute session is NZD155 and five are NZD750. Both packages include three guided practice MP3s. Prices include GST. Selectable text states the package totals separately from single-session prices and records the source date. The cards remain available as local resources. No calendar or availability has been invented.

“Enquire about a session” opens an editable mail-app draft to the source's published address. The draft asks for preferred date/time, time zone, online/in-person and session length; phone is optional. The visitor must deliberately send it. This does not reserve a session, take payment or confirm a booking. Booking remains a later feature.
